Who it’s for?

Beginners are welcome, and so are returning meditators. This is for anyone who wants steadiness, clarity, and inner depth — without strain, performance, or spiritual jargon.

Your 7DMC Syllabus(being finalised)

  1. Day 1: Welcome, foundations — the One Rule of Meditation, sitting, posture, the device, and our first practice together.
  2. Day 2: Working with mind-wandering
  3. Day 3: Dealing with a chatty mind, surfacing emotions, movements of energy in and around your body
  4. Day 4: Self-witnessing - the real meaning behind the phrase "being in the world, but not of it". Self & ego.
  5. Day 5: Mindfulness & dynamic awareness
  6. Day 6: Meditation and lifestyle. What to expect as you engage long term with meditation
  7. Day 7: Integration — a practice you can keep, going forward

FAQ

Do I need meditation experience?

No. This is beginner-friendly, and also useful for returning meditators who want fresh simplicity.

What if I miss a day?

Simply continue the next day. At the end of each session I will send you a brief recap and links to cheatsheets and videos.

Is this therapy?

No — this is meditation training and contemplative practice. If you’re in a tender space, you’re welcome. We keep the approach grounded and supportive.

Any other questions?

Feel free to email me and I will respond within 12 hours.
