FAQ

Do I need meditation experience?

No. This is beginner-friendly, and also useful for returning meditators who want fresh simplicity.

What if I miss a day?

Simply continue the next day. At the end of each session I will send you a brief recap and links to cheatsheets and videos.

Is this therapy?

No — this is meditation training and contemplative practice. If you’re in a tender space, you’re welcome. We keep the approach grounded and supportive.
