I teach direct, first-hand experience of Self and spiritual realms — meditation beyond belief, into knowing. This grows out of over [forty years](https://petertwigg.com/2026/05/03/the-three-phases-of-spiritual-awakening/) of my own practice and thirty years of teaching it, in retreats, workshops and lectures across Australia, Asia, Europe and the USA. My background is also clinical having been a former homoeopath and naturopath.

My training is rooted in the Western Esoteric Tradition through the Clairvision School of Samuel Sagan — a lineage that maps consciousness beginning with [the fourfold subtle body model](https://petertwigg.com/context/2020/03/01/physical-and-spiritual-bodies-in-meditation/) and orients practice around [Truth as a living, direct presence](https://petertwigg.com/text/2026/01/25/truth-as-living-presence/) rather than concept. The tradition's alchemical language points the same way — the two movements of [VITRIOL and INRI](https://petertwigg.com/2026/07/28/into-the-earth-through-the-fire/), dissolving what's false and building what's true. The School of Knowing, which I founded in 2023, is my current vehicle for teaching within this lineage.

I am the author of *[Truth Centred Meditation](https://petertwigg.com/books/)* (Cauthray Publishing, 2023) — the systematic frame behind the practice, drawn from forty years of practice and thirty years of teaching.
