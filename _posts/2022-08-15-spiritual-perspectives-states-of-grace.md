What is your experience of states of grace in meditation? Recognising states of grace in your meditation helps you in your meditations and life.

Tips, techniques & context to help improve your meditation.

Grace doesn't only descend on the cushion. Sometimes it arrives in [the middle of an ordinary day](https://petertwigg.com/2026/05/03/grace-in-daily-life/), in the pause that opens between the busy and the still. And when grace meets the broken places, [the meditation work of mending a broken heart](https://petertwigg.com/2026/03/19/mending-a-broken-heart/) becomes a path of its own.

If recognising and receiving grace is part of the practice you want to build, the [School of Knowing](https://petertwigg.com/school-of-knowing/) is a year-long course in exactly this work.
